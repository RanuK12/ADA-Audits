Case Study

Real Results

FinanceFlow — DeFi Platform

Industry: Decentralized Finance (DeFi) · B2C SaaS

The Problem

FinanceFlow, a fast-growing DeFi lending platform with 120,000+ monthly active users, received a demand letter citing 23 ADA Title III violations on their web application. Their legal counsel estimated exposure of $150,000–$300,000 in damages and legal fees. The platform had zero accessibility testing in place.

What We Found

Our comprehensive audit uncovered 47 WCAG 2.1 AA failures across their core user flows:

  • Critical: Trading dashboard was completely inaccessible to screen readers — blind users could not execute any transactions
  • Critical: Color-only error indicators on loan forms (red/green) failed 4.5:1 contrast ratios, excluding 8% of male users with color vision deficiency
  • High: Keyboard traps in the wallet connection modal — keyboard-only users were locked out of the platform entirely
  • High: Missing ARIA labels on 34 interactive components, breaking assistive technology navigation
  • Medium: No skip navigation, no focus management on dynamic content updates, missing form field descriptions

The Solution

We delivered a prioritized remediation roadmap with code-level fixes for all 47 issues. Our team worked alongside their frontend developers over 3 weeks to implement the changes, including a full ARIA landmark restructure, keyboard navigation overhaul, and contrast-compliant design system update.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I really need an ADA audit?

If your business has a website or web app that serves US or EU customers, yes. ADA Title III applies to all businesses open to the public. The EAA (European Accessibility Act) takes effect June 2025 and covers digital services across the EU. Proactive compliance is always cheaper than reactive litigation.

How long does the audit take?

Starter audits are delivered within 3 business days. Professional audits take 5 business days. Enterprise timelines depend on scope — we'll give you a clear timeline upfront.

Can you fix the issues too?

Absolutely. We offer remediation services for all audit tiers. Our team can implement fixes directly or provide detailed guidance for your developers. Remediation is quoted separately based on scope.

What standards do you test against?

We test against WCAG 2.1 Level AA (the global standard), ADA Title III requirements, Section 508 (for US government), and the European Accessibility Act (EAA). Enterprise plans also cover WCAG 2.2 and AAA criteria.

Is a one-time audit enough?

For compliance documentation, yes. But websites change constantly. We recommend quarterly re-audits to maintain compliance. Our Enterprise plan includes ongoing monitoring and quarterly reviews.
