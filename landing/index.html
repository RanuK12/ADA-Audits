FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ADA compliance audits.

How long does an ADA accessibility audit take?

Our standard Professional audit is delivered within 48 hours. Essential audits are typically completed in 24 hours. Enterprise monitoring runs continuously.

How much does a WCAG audit cost?

Our Essential Audit starts at $899 one-time, Professional Audit at $1,499 one-time, and Enterprise Monitoring at $2,999/month. No hidden fees.

What is WCAG 2.1 AA compliance?

WCAG 2.1 AA is the web accessibility standard required by ADA and Section 508. It covers perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust criteria that ensure websites are accessible to people with disabilities.

Can you fix the issues too?

Our audit includes a detailed report with prioritized fixes. While we don't do the dev work directly, our reports are designed so your development team can implement changes quickly. We also offer fix verification as part of our Professional and Enterprise plans.
