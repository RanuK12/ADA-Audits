Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I really need an ADA audit?

If your business has a website or web app that serves US or EU customers, yes. ADA Title III applies to all businesses open to the public. The EAA (European Accessibility Act) takes effect June 2025 and covers digital services across the EU. Proactive compliance is always cheaper than reactive litigation.

How long does the audit take?

Starter audits are delivered within 3 business days. Professional audits take 5 business days. Enterprise timelines depend on scope — we'll give you a clear timeline upfront.

Can you fix the issues too?

Absolutely. We offer remediation services for all audit tiers. Our team can implement fixes directly or provide detailed guidance for your developers. Remediation is quoted separately based on scope.

What standards do you test against?

We test against WCAG 2.1 Level AA (the global standard), ADA Title III requirements, Section 508 (for US government), and the European Accessibility Act (EAA). Enterprise plans also cover WCAG 2.2 and AAA criteria.

Is a one-time audit enough?

For compliance documentation, yes. But websites change constantly. We recommend quarterly re-audits to maintain compliance. Our Enterprise plan includes ongoing monitoring and quarterly reviews.
